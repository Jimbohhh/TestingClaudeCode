Questions

What's free and what costs money?

Every build's plans are free: the parts list, printer settings and step-by-step instructions. You can print them or save them as a PDF. The ready-to-print STL files are a small one-time purchase.

How do I get my files after I pay?

Checkout is handled by Stripe. Right after you pay, the confirmation page shows your download link, and Stripe emails you a receipt.

What printer do I need?

Any FDM printer with a bed of at least 180 × 180 mm works for every build here. Settings in the plans assume a 0.4 mm nozzle.

Can I sell what I print?

Files are for personal and classroom use. If you'd like to sell prints, get in touch about a maker license.
